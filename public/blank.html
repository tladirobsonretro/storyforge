Story Workshop · Step 3

Plan your season

Turn the Series Bible into a season-long journey. Give the season a beginning, a middle that escalates and an ending that changes something.

Season plan · draft
1. The season promise

What will the audience come back for, and what question will keep them watching?

2. Episode map

Start with eight episode slots as a planning scaffold. You can change the episode count and revise the outline later. Each episode needs its own goal, obstacle, turning point and reason to watch the next one.

Keep this as story planning for now. Detailed scripts and shot breakdowns come later.

Planning rule: Every episode should move at least one major story forward. The outline should fit your audience, format and available production capacity. Do not split the story into 30-second clips yet; first make sure the season works as a story.

Step 4 can later break each approved episode into scenes and shots for production.
